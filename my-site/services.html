Services

Eight automation services. One approach: understand the workflow first, then build the smallest system that removes the most work.

Customer-facing channels

Answer every message, on every channel, immediately

Most businesses do not lose customers on price. They lose them on silence. These four services close that gap.

01

Instagram automation

Turn your Instagram inbox from a backlog into a working sales channel. Conversations get answered in seconds, and every serious enquiry ends up somewhere you can act on it.

  • DM auto-replies that read intent, not keywords
  • Comment and story-reply triggers
  • Product, price and stock answers from your own data
  • Lead capture straight into a sheet or CRM
  • Handover to a human with the full conversation attached
  • Instagram Graph API
  • n8n
  • CRM sync
02

WhatsApp Business automation

The channel your customers actually use, wired into your operations. Order status, reminders and support handled automatically — with clean escalation when a person is needed.

  • Order and delivery status from your live records
  • Appointment and payment reminders
  • Support triage with routing rules per topic
  • Opt-in broadcast campaigns
  • Full conversation history in one place
  • WhatsApp Business API
  • Templates
  • Escalation rules
03

Facebook Messenger automation

Conversation flows that behave like your best salesperson on a slow day: patient with questions, quick to qualify, and honest about what it does not know.

  • Guided flows for the questions you get most
  • Qualification questions before a human is involved
  • Routing by product, region or language
  • Persistent menu and quick replies
  • Messenger Platform
  • Lead routing
04

Email automation

An inbox that sorts itself. Enquiries classified, logged and answered — with drafts prepared from your real pricing and waiting for your approval.

  • Classification by intent, value and urgency
  • Auto-responders that set expectations honestly
  • Follow-up sequences that stop when someone replies
  • Draft quotes built from your price list
  • Every enquiry logged and searchable
  • Gmail / IMAP
  • Approval step
  • Sequences
Behind the scenes

The work nobody sees, done without anyone doing it

Data entry, filing, chasing, reporting. This is usually where the biggest hours are hiding.

05

AI agent workflows

Agents that read a request, decide what it is, and act — inside limits you set. Useful where the rules are clear but the inputs are messy.

  • Decisions grounded in your data, not guesswork
  • Explicit boundaries and fallback behaviour
  • Human approval on anything expensive or irreversible
  • Full logs of what ran and why
  • LLM tooling
  • Guardrails
  • Audit logs
06

Document automation

Invoices, delivery notes, purchase orders and forms — read, extracted and filed automatically, then turned into the reports you currently build by hand.

  • Field extraction from PDFs, scans and photos
  • Validation rules before anything is written
  • Rows pushed to Sheets, Airtable or a database
  • Scheduled reports and digests
  • Parsing
  • Validation
  • Reporting
07

Lead handling automation

One pipeline for every channel. Leads are captured, de-duplicated, scored against your criteria and delivered to the right person while they are still warm.

  • Capture from DMs, forms, WhatsApp, email and ads
  • De-duplication across channels
  • Scoring on your own signals
  • Instant alerts on high-value enquiries
  • Multi-channel
  • Scoring
  • Alerts
08

Internal workflow automation (n8n)

The connective tissue. n8n workflows that move data between the tools you already pay for, on a schedule or on a trigger, without anyone re-typing anything.

  • Tool-to-tool sync over APIs and webhooks
  • Scheduled jobs, retries and error alerts
  • Approval steps where a human must sign off
  • Runs in your own n8n instance — you own it
  • n8n
  • Webhooks
  • REST APIs
