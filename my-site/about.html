About

Who

Building automation that survives a real week

I'm Tarik Dadda. I build custom AI automation systems for businesses in Algeria — connecting the tools you already use into workflows that run on their own.

Most of my work starts the same way: a business is growing, the messages have outgrown the people answering them, and the team is spending its best hours on the same forty questions and the same copy-paste between two screens.

My job is to remove that work without breaking anything customers can see. That means understanding your process before touching a tool, being blunt about what automation will not fix, and leaving you with a system your team can actually operate.

I work with small and medium businesses, distributors, importers, wholesalers, service companies and local shops — usually remotely, across the country.

How we work

Five principles, applied to every project

These are not slogans. They are the reasons projects here tend to land, and the things I will push back on if a brief ignores them.

Listen before building

Every project starts with your actual process — who touches what, where it breaks, and what a good week looks like. No assumptions, no discovery theatre.

Build for reality

Systems are designed for your busiest day, your slowest supplier and your least technical team member — not for a clean demo.

Train your team

A system nobody understands is a liability. Every handover includes documentation in plain language and a session with whoever will run it.

Iterate after launch

Real customers always find the edge case. The first version is a starting point — we watch how it behaves against actual messages and tighten it from there, rather than declaring victory at handover.

Automate honestly

No inflated claims about what AI can do, no black boxes, and no automation where a person is genuinely better. If a task should not be automated yet, I will tell you that on the first call instead of selling you a project.
