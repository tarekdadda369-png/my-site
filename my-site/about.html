Building automation that works

TARIK DADDA builds custom AI automation systems for businesses in Algeria. We specialize in connecting the tools you already use — Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, email, and internal systems — into intelligent workflows that run on their own.

Our approach is simple: understand your business first, then build automation that solves real problems. We do not sell pre-made templates or force you into a platform that does not fit. Every system is designed around how your team actually works.

We work with small and medium businesses, distributors, importers, wholesalers, service businesses, and local companies across Algeria. Whether you need to automate customer responses, streamline document handling, or build complex internal workflows with n8n, we deliver systems that are reliable, maintainable, and built for your specific needs.
