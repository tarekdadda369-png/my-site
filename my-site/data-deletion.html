User Data Deletion Instructions

Service AI respects your privacy and your right to control your personal data.

How to request deletion

If you have connected your Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, or WhatsApp account to our services and wish to delete your personal data, please send an email to:

Email: support@service.co.im

Subject: User Data Deletion Request

Please include

  • Your full name
  • Your Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp account associated with the request
  • Your email address (if applicable)
  • A brief description of the data you want deleted

What happens next?

After verifying your request, we will remove your personal data from our systems within 30 days unless we are legally required to retain certain records.

Once completed, you will receive confirmation by email.
