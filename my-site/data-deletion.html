User data deletion

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. Here is exactly how to have it deleted.

Last updated · 26 July 2026

In one line: email contact@service.co.im with the subject User Data Deletion Request, and your data is removed within 30 days.

How to request deletion

If you have connected your Facebook, Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp account to our services and wish to delete your personal data, send us an email:

  • Email: contact@service.co.im
  • Subject: User Data Deletion Request

Please include

  1. Your full name.
  2. The Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account associated with the request.
  3. Your email address, if it differs from the one you are writing from.
  4. A brief description of the data you want deleted.

We ask for these details only to confirm the request comes from you and to locate the right records. They are not used for anything else.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your request, usually within one business day.
  2. We verify the request so that nobody can delete someone else's data.
  3. We remove your personal data from our systems within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain certain records.
  4. You receive written confirmation by email once the deletion is complete.

What this covers

A deletion request covers the personal data held in our own systems as part of an automation we operate or have built. It does not delete data held by Meta, Google or any other platform under their own terms — for those, use the platform's own privacy settings and deletion tools.

If you are a business client and want an entire automation and its stored data removed, say so in the message and we will confirm the scope with you before deleting anything irreversible.

Privacy contact

For any privacy question, including this process:

  • Email: contact@service.co.im
  • Website: service.co.im
  • Location: Algeria
